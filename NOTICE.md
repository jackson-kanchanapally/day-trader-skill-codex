## Software

This project adapts Jake Schincariol's [day-trader-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/day-trader-skill). The original MIT copyright notice remains in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Original US utilities retained unchanged under `legacy/` supply the reused Wilder RSI and selected scanner, entry, exit, risk, alert and RSS rules. Explicit Indian adapters live in `workflow/`. Modifications include Indian-market providers and calculations, restored file-handoff commands, local paper accounting, Codex coordination, research tooling and the React dashboard.

React, React DOM, Vite and their transitive dependencies retain their own licenses and notices. Dependency versions are locked in `dashboard/package-lock.json`; installation obtains their package license files. The animated agent desk illustrations are implemented in this repository as SVG/React components, without downloaded avatar artwork.

## Market data, filings and news

The software license does not relicense third-party responses, prices, exchange/security directories, company filings, news descriptions or other provider content. The public source profile generated by `scripts/build_release.py` includes only the original synthetic inputs declared in `release-manifest.json` and documented in [fixture provenance](tests/fixtures/README.md). Provider-shaped test payloads are generated from original arithmetic sequences, without downloaded prices.

The development checkout and its Git history may also contain captured inputs under `tests/fixtures/india/` and `tests/fixtures/trader-*/`. The public exporter excludes those inputs, their dependent historical tests and Git history. Filenames or labels such as `live`, `captured`, or a real ticker are not grants of redistribution permission. Generating an export does not remove historical blobs or establish permission to publish the development history.

# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Add dashboard switches for Telegram alert streams (**Alert trades → Telegram alert streams**). Each switch is saved in `var/state/alert-switches.json`, which the dashboard and the `--serve` scheduler share, and takes effect on the next 30-second tick. **Gold futures signals** (on by default, so current behaviour is unchanged) mutes the gold morning alert, live level updates, failure notices and gold lab messages. While it is off, no new gold paper trade is opened, but the gold lab still logs its forward decisions and Telegram commands are still answered. **Index F&O signals** (off by default) turns on the new `india/index_fno.py`: a paper-only NIFTY 50 / NIFTY BANK opening-range breakout signal on the recorder's sampled five-minute NSE index candles, with a range-width filter, an India VIX ceiling, a stop at the other side of the range, half booked at 1R with the stop moved to the entry, the rest at 2R or a 15:15 square-off, and a Telegram update for each step. It starts an indices-only recorder when `recorder.enabled` is false. Levels are on the spot index from NSE's delayed snapshot; there is no backtest yet. See [Index F&O signals](docs/TELEGRAM_ALERTS.md#index-fo-signals).
- Add the gold strategy lab, `python3 -m india.gold_lab` ([docs/GOLD_LAB.md](docs/GOLD_LAB.md)). 21 strategy configurations (buy and hold, the morning alert's swing rules, time-series momentum, EMA trend, Donchian breakouts, RSI(2) dip buying, a dollar-gated momentum, a trend ensemble and two post-hoc blends) run in one engine. The engine trades whole GOLDPETAL lots with volatility targeting, next-open fills, MCX costs, monthly rolls and futures carry. `--research` reports 2011+ test-year results, a yearly walk-forward, block-bootstrap Sharpe comparisons and a multiple-testing haircut. With `gold.enabled`, `--serve` logs every strategy's daily decision after the gold alert to a hash-chained ledger (`var/state/gold_lab/decisions.jsonl`) and replays forward paper accounts from it. It sends a Telegram message when the champion's position changes and a weekly scoreboard, and promotes a challenger only under fixed rules (40 forward sessions, a +0.10 Sharpe edge, bootstrap P ≥ 0.80, ≤ 5% replay disagreement). `/lab` shows the scoreboard. `scripts/gold_lab_improve.sh` (`python3 -m india.gold_lab_improve`) runs a monthly improvement with any server-hosted AI: pi, Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Aider, a custom command, or an OpenAI-compatible cloud API such as DeepSeek. The AI may only add challenger files under `india/gold_lab_challengers/` and append to the journal. In an isolated worktree, the runner enforces that diff policy, the look-ahead test, the test suite and a research gate, then commits on a new branch without merging. Local models are refused. First run: no strategy is proven better than buy and hold (best P = 0.66 before the post-hoc blends). The swing rules and the blends kept most of its return with 4–7% drawdowns against 37% when cash earns interest. With idle cash, carry cut 1× futures buy and hold from 10.9% to 3.3% a year.
- The gold alert is now one short card: the verdict, price, a five-line backdrop (trend, macro, news, funds, fear), then the plan or the watch levels, two headlines and a footer. `/news` keeps the full brief. A replay of a past date no longer shows today's live price.
- Gold alerts consider the news. `india/gold_news.py` scores 48 hours of free headlines (Google News RSS for global gold, Fed, dollar, yields and geopolitics plus Indian MCX, rupee, RBI and duty news; the Economic Times commodities RSS) with a transparent rule list, and adds CFTC managed-money positioning and a VIX/S&P 500 risk mood. Each alert shows the news flow, top headlines with links, a five-vote backdrop and a conviction rating per setup, and flags setups against the news (`gold.news`: `flag`, `skip` or `off`). `/news` is a new command. MCX futures prices quoted in the news now set the basis when there is no fresh calibration. The default basis is 1.15 instead of 1.06, which put levels about 9% too low. A positioning filter was tested and not adopted.
- Gold alerts: the morning message adds a delayed live price and warns when overnight COMEX trading is already past a setup's trigger or no-chase level. `--serve` now sends live level alerts for gold paper trades during MCX hours (near trigger, trigger, T1, stop; `live_watch`, `watch_minutes`, `mcx_hours`) and answers `/gold`, `/price`, `/trades`, `/calibrate` and `/help` from the alert chat (`commands`). Five regime filters were tested and none was adopted (see [docs/GOLD_ALERTS.md](docs/GOLD_ALERTS.md)).
- Add `python3 -m india.gold`: a morning MCX GOLD PETAL swing alert on the existing Telegram bot (`gold` settings, off by default; `--serve` runs it Monday–Friday at 08:30 IST). Prices are a free rupees-per-gram proxy, COMEX gold × USD/INR × a basis that `--calibrate PRICE` sets from the broker's GOLDPETAL price. Trend-pullback and 20-day-breakout setups need weekly-trend agreement and are scored with ADX, MACD, RSI, the dollar index and US yields. Plans give an SL-order entry, a structure/ATR stop, T1 at 1R, a 2 ATR trailing exit, a 40-session time stop and lots sized to the rupee risk with MCX cost estimates. Every sent setup is paper-traded, with progress in later messages. `--backtest` replays the rules since 2006: +0.20R per trade over 106 long trades, but random long entries with the same exits did about as well, so the entries show no proven edge (see [docs/GOLD_ALERTS.md](docs/GOLD_ALERTS.md)).
- Add an optional intraday recorder (`india/recorder.py`, `recorder` settings, off by default) that runs inside `india.picks --serve` and stores evidence under `var/`. It samples NIFTY 50, NIFTY BANK, India VIX and the sector indices from the NSE official MCP every 30 seconds into labelled five-minute candles, and records five-minute stock closes for the alert universe from Yahoo once per completed window (20 symbols per call, in sequence). Each source backs off on its own, nothing is retried immediately, and missing samples stay missing. Index candles carry a strict `complete` flag and a looser `usable` flag for close-based checks. The NSE stock snapshot is kept as an opt-in feed because one call covers about a tenth of the NIFTY 200. `--record-once --dry-run` checks a source without Telegram secrets or saved data. No trade signals or Telegram messages are added. See [Intraday recorder](docs/TELEGRAM_ALERTS.md#intraday-recorder).
- Preserve `--dry-run` in the scheduler's recursive morning/settle jobs.
- Add `python3 -m india.picks`: a daily Telegram bot alert with the top three NSE intraday setups (volume, EMA trend, MACD, RSI, ATR), each with entry, exit and cost-inclusive risk plans. It skips holidays and late wakes, sends once per session and reports failures to the chat. See [docs/TELEGRAM_ALERTS.md](docs/TELEGRAM_ALERTS.md).
- Paper-trade every sent alert on 5-minute candles (`--settle`, every 10 minutes via LaunchAgent) with an end-of-day Telegram scorecard and a new dashboard **Alert trades** page (`/api/alert-trades`). `--dry-run` no longer writes the alert record. A temporary data outage keeps each trade's last good result (marked stale) and delays the scorecard until data returns, or until 18:00 IST.
- Explain every alert paper trade (`india/review.py`): failed breakout, stop too tight, gap or NIFTY against the trade, no follow-through and similar, with excursions in R, in the scorecard and dashboard. Setup traits that lose over at least 8 closed trades become lessons that lower future alert scores (`learning`, `learn_min_trades`, `learn_penalty`, `learn_max_penalty`). Execution patterns are reported as suggestions only.
- Skip alert setups with results or another material board meeting from the signal session through the trade day, or a split/bonus-type ex-date on the trade day, using NSE's public event calendar and corporate-action lists (`india/events.py`, `"events": "skip" | "flag" | "off"`). Dividend ex-dates and minor meetings are flagged. A cached calendar is used if NSE is unreachable; otherwise the alert says the check could not run.
- Send in-session Telegram updates for each alert paper trade: trigger, T1, final exit with net P&L, and gap skips or no trigger by the deadline (`live_updates`). Each is sent once; late milestones are recorded silently and the scorecard covers them. `settle_minutes` (5–30) sets the `--serve` and LaunchAgent settle cadence.
- Add `python3 -m india.picks_backtest`: it replays the alert scorer over about four years of daily candles. Each trade is bracketed by the worse and better intraday path, and the last ~60 days are replayed exactly on 5-minute candles. It reports results by score bucket and by trait with standard errors. The first run found no edge after costs (see docs/TELEGRAM_ALERTS.md).
- Learning lessons now also need the trait's gap to exceed two standard errors. Worse-looking traits inside the noise show as "Within noise".
- Add `docker-compose.yml` + `Dockerfile.app`: always-on `dashboard` and `alerts` (`india.picks --serve`) containers with health checks; `python3 -m dashboard --host` for container binding.

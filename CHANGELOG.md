# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The index options desk trades like a professional. Defaults move to the 30-minute opening range, a stop where the breakout is proven wrong (`stop_mode` "candle") and a trailing runner after T1 (`runner` "trail"): in a 60-session engine replay every breakout went from +0.04R to about +0.15R per trade (not yet significant). Account rules: one loss a day, a weekly stop at −3R, half size after 2 straight losses or 5R of drawdown, all from the desk's own closed trades. The brief adds overnight global cues (shown, not traded on), the desk's record and today's size; the evening note adds a journal. The learned model is on probation: it decides only once it beats every breakout and at most 5% of shuffled-label runs, checked after each session. Memory was rebuilt on the new rules (83 breakouts); the 15-minute dataset is kept as `dataset-orb15-backup.jsonl`.
- Walk-forward learning for the index options desk (`python3 -m india.index_fno_learn`). The last ~60 sessions are replayed oldest first through the live engine, five minutes at a time, so a 10:05 decision sees only what was known at 10:05. Every breakout is paper-traded to its stop, target, time stop or square-off. A logistic model trained only on earlier days' finished trades predicts each breakout's chance of a win and TAKEs it at 0.55 or more; the day's outcomes join the training set after the close. Signals now record 14 features at signal time (trend alignment, CPR, range, VIX and its change, candle body, room to the next level, time, gap, move from the open in ATR, ATR, breakout extension, side, index). The report compares every breakout, the current grade rule and the model on the same days, scores the predictions against a no-skill forecast, and runs a shuffled-label control. First run (59 sessions, 106 breakouts): model TAKE +0.12R ± 0.10 over 32 trades against +0.05R for every breakout, but 16% of shuffled runs did as well and its Brier score was worse than no skill, so nothing learnable was found. The model is not used by the live alerts.
- The options desk keeps a learning memory and decides with it. `var/state/index_fno/learning/dataset.jsonl` holds every learned breakout (signal-time features, outcome, the live decision), seeded with the 60-day walk (`--seed`, 106 breakouts); `model.json` is retrained on all of it. With `index_fno.learning: "decide"` (default) a breakout is taken only at P(win) ≥ `learn_threshold` (0.55) once the model has `learn_min_trades` (25); the grade and the <1R level rule become notes, and risk, VIX, event and option checks still apply. Skips say `SKIPPED_MODEL` and whether the grade rule would have taken it; alerts show P(win). `"advise"` shows P(win) while the rules decide; `"off"` ignores the model. `--serve` runs `india.index_fno_learn --update` 25 minutes after the square-off: today's session is replayed, every breakout (taken or not) is stored with its outcome, the model is retrained, and a Telegram note reports what was learned and the live model's record. Memory grows past Yahoo's 60-day window; `--report` re-runs the walk-forward test over all of it.
- Index options use the Greeks. `options.bs_greeks` gives delta, gamma, theta (per day) and vega (per IV point); the free model returns all four and the Upstox chain keeps its gamma, theta and vega. Each candidate structure must have net delta of at least `min_delta` (0.10) and pay at most `max_theta_share` (25%) of its T1 premium gain in theta by the time stop. IV is cut by `iv_shock_pct` (5%) where the index rallies (a call's targets, a put's stop), so vega lowers reward/risk and lot size. Alerts add the position's Greeks in rupees (delta → delta at T1, ₹ per point, theta per day and to the time stop, vega and the IV-drop cost, points per hour to cover theta); the time stop reports theta paid; the brief adds ATM call/put Greeks per index, compared with the ATR pace. Replay over 59 sessions: the edge is unchanged (+0.02R ± 0.05 over 106 breakouts); in live rules the one trade the old rules took (a Δ≈0.09 debit spread that lost 0.58R) is now refused.
- Offline mornings no longer lose the day.
  - **Brief:** the index options brief waits for the daily data and is sent late (marked `LATE`, with the opening ranges) until the entry deadline.
  - **Missing candles:** recorder gaps are filled from Yahoo's free five-minute candles across the whole gap, so a breakout that happened while offline is seen as stale and not chased, while later breakouts are traded.
  - **Messages:** Telegram messages go through an outbox in the day's state. Failed sends are retried, late ones are marked "Delayed N min", and stale entries expire with their follow-ups and a note.
  - **Daily jobs:** `--serve` spends an attempt of the morning picks, gold alert and gold lab only when Telegram is reachable (`internet_ok`), so they still go out after a late connection.
- Index options: a daily risk guard (`max_open_trades`, default 1, across indices; `max_daily_loss`, default 2 × the options risk per trade, counting realised losses and open risk, with new trades sized to the room left) and `python3 -m india.index_fno_backtest`, which replays the live engine over the last ~60 sessions on Yahoo five-minute index and VIX candles. The first replay (59 sessions, 106 breakouts) found no edge: +0.02R ± 0.05 per trade. The 60-minute time stop closed 92 of 106 trades because 1R was reached within an hour only 6% of the time, and the "level within 1R" blocker stopped 81% of breakouts without improving results.
- Index F&O signals become an index options desk (`india/index_fno.py`, `india/options.py`). A 09:05 brief gives yesterday's levels, CPR, R1/S1, the daily trend, ATR, the VIX regime and the expiry in use, plus PCR, max pain and OI walls with a live chain. Opening-range breakouts need a buffered close and a grade of at least 60 from trend, CPR, room to the next level (under 1R blocks the trade), candle strength, range quality, VIX and PCR. Each alert names the contract (ATM or ITM buy, or a debit spread at least four strikes wide, chosen by reward to T2 per rupee risked after charges, at least 1.5) with entry, do-not-chase, premium and spot stops, T1/T2, a 60-minute theta time stop and lots sized to `risk_per_trade`. Updates report exit premiums and rupee P&L after charges. Everything is free and needs no broker account. Premiums are Black-Scholes estimates from NSE's India VIX, with BANKNIFTY's IV scaled by its realised-volatility ratio to NIFTY. Alerts lead with index levels, show premiums as "≈" and give the delta. An Upstox live chain is opt-in only (`chain_source: "upstox"`). Expiries avoid sessions under `min_dte`. Skipped breakouts send one line with the reason. No backtest.
- Add dashboard switches for Telegram alert streams (**Alert trades → Telegram alert streams**). Each switch is saved in `var/state/alert-switches.json`, which the dashboard and the `--serve` scheduler share, and takes effect on the next 30-second tick. **Gold futures signals** (on by default, so current behaviour is unchanged) mutes the gold morning alert, live level updates, failure notices and gold lab messages. While it is off, no new gold paper trade is opened, but the gold lab still logs its forward decisions and Telegram commands are still answered. **Index F&O signals** (off by default) turns on the new `india/index_fno.py`: a paper-only NIFTY 50 / NIFTY BANK opening-range breakout signal on the recorder's sampled five-minute NSE index candles, with a range-width filter, an India VIX ceiling, a stop at the other side of the range, half booked at 1R with the stop moved to the entry, the rest at 2R or a 15:15 square-off, and a Telegram update for each step. It starts an indices-only recorder when `recorder.enabled` is false. Levels are on the spot index from NSE's delayed snapshot; there is no backtest yet. See [Index options signals](docs/TELEGRAM_ALERTS.md#index-options-signals).
- Add the gold strategy lab, `python3 -m india.gold_lab` ([docs/GOLD_LAB.md](docs/GOLD_LAB.md)). 21 strategy configurations (buy and hold, the morning alert's swing rules, time-series momentum, EMA trend, Donchian breakouts, RSI(2) dip buying, a dollar-gated momentum, a trend ensemble and two post-hoc blends) run in one engine. The engine trades whole GOLDPETAL lots with volatility targeting, next-open fills, MCX costs, monthly rolls and futures carry. `--research` reports 2011+ test-year results, a yearly walk-forward, block-bootstrap Sharpe comparisons and a multiple-testing haircut. With `gold.enabled`, `--serve` logs every strategy's daily decision after the gold alert to a hash-chained ledger (`var/state/gold_lab/decisions.jsonl`) and replays forward paper accounts from it. It sends a Telegram message when the champion's position changes and a weekly scoreboard, and promotes a challenger only under fixed rules (40 forward sessions, a +0.10 Sharpe edge, bootstrap P ≥ 0.80, ≤ 5% replay disagreement). `/lab` shows the scoreboard. `scripts/gold_lab_improve.sh` (`python3 -m india.gold_lab_improve`) runs a monthly improvement with any server-hosted AI: pi, Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Aider, a custom command, or an OpenAI-compatible cloud API such as DeepSeek. The AI may only add challenger files under `india/gold_lab_challengers/` and append to the journal. In an isolated worktree, the runner enforces that diff policy, the look-ahead test, the test suite and a research gate, then commits on a new branch without merging. Local models are refused. First run: no strategy is proven better than buy and hold (best P = 0.66 before the post-hoc blends). The swing rules and the blends kept most of its return with 4–7% drawdowns against 37% when cash earns interest. With idle cash, carry cut 1× futures buy and hold from 10.9% to 3.3% a year.
- The gold alert is now one short card: the verdict, price, a five-line backdrop (trend, macro, news, funds, fear), then the plan or the watch levels, two headlines and a footer. `/news` keeps the full brief. A replay of a past date no longer shows today's live price.
- Gold alerts consider the news. `india/gold_news.py` scores 48 hours of free headlines (Google News RSS for global gold, Fed, dollar, yields and geopolitics plus Indian MCX, rupee, RBI and duty news; the Economic Times commodities RSS) with a transparent rule list, and adds CFTC managed-money positioning and a VIX/S&P 500 risk mood. Each alert shows the news flow, top headlines with links, a five-vote backdrop and a conviction rating per setup, and flags setups against the news (`gold.news`: `flag`, `skip` or `off`). `/news` is a new command. MCX futures prices quoted in the news now set the basis when there is no fresh calibration. The default basis is 1.15 instead of 1.06, which put levels about 9% too low. A positioning filter was tested and not adopted.
- Gold alerts: the morning message adds a delayed live price and warns when overnight COMEX trading is already past a setup's trigger or no-chase level. `--serve` now sends live level alerts for gold paper trades during MCX hours (near trigger, trigger, T1, stop; `live_watch`, `watch_minutes`, `mcx_hours`) and answers `/gold`, `/price`, `/trades`, `/calibrate` and `/help` from the alert chat (`commands`). Five regime filters were tested and none was adopted (see [docs/GOLD_ALERTS.md](docs/GOLD_ALERTS.md)).
- Add `python3 -m india.gold`: a morning MCX GOLD PETAL swing alert on the existing Telegram bot (`gold` settings, off by default; `--serve` runs it Monday–Friday at 08:30 IST). Prices are a free rupees-per-gram proxy, COMEX gold × USD/INR × a basis that `--calibrate PRICE` sets from the broker's GOLDPETAL price. Trend-pullback and 20-day-breakout setups need weekly-trend agreement and are scored with ADX, MACD, RSI, the dollar index and US yields. Every sent setup is paper-traded, with progress in later messages. `--backtest` replays the rules since 2006: +0.20R per trade over 106 long trades, but random long entries with the same exits did about as well, so the entries show no proven edge (see [docs/GOLD_ALERTS.md](docs/GOLD_ALERTS.md)).
